This page explains what happens to information when you use Guess Paper Builder. It is written in plain language. Last updated 2 November 2026.

## Summary

- We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name or email to use the tool.
- Your uploaded files are read in your browser. They are not sent to our servers.
- Your AI API key stays in your browser memory for the current session. It is not saved to a database or sent to us.
- When you ask the tool to generate a guess paper or answer a question, the relevant text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, using your key.
- We show advertising through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your past visits.

## Files you upload

Files are processed in your browser using JavaScript. We do not receive copies of them. Closing or refreshing the tab removes them from the page.

## API keys

Your API key is held in memory in your current browser tab. We do not save it in cookies, local storage or a database, and we do not transmit it to any server operated by us. Your requests go straight to the provider you selected: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Analytics and server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel may keep standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and reliability. We do not add tracking scripts of our own.

## Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can manage personalised advertising in your Google account settings, or opt out of certain third-party cookies at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

## Children

The tool is intended for students. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to review this policy before you use the tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
