Guess Paper Builder is a free study tool for students. It reads past exam papers and a textbook, finds the questions that keep repeating, and helps you build a practice paper from them.

## Why it exists

I was preparing for my intermediate exams with about ten past papers from the last five years and my own book. I could not see clearly which questions were coming back, and going through everything by hand took more time than I had. I built this tool for myself, and I am sharing it so other students do not have to start from zero.

## What it does

- Reads PDF, Word, Excel, CSV and text files in your browser.
- Splits papers into questions and groups similar ones.
- Produces a repetition report showing how often each group appears and in which years.
- Writes a guess paper with sections and an answer key, using an AI provider you choose.
- Answers follow-up questions about your material in any language.

## How it is built

The tool is a React and TypeScript web app. It runs in your browser and uses your own API key to talk to the AI provider you select. There are no accounts, and the tool does not store your files on a server. The code is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/ubaidslab/guess-paper-builder).

## Who it is for

Intermediate and board students, teachers who want to see recurring exam patterns, and self-learners who study without a coaching centre.
