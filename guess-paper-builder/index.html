How it works

Three steps from a pile of past papers to a practice paper.

  1. 1

    Upload

    Add your past papers and your book. Each file is read directly in your browser, so nothing is stored on our side.

  2. 2

    Analyse

    Questions are split out and grouped by similarity. You see which questions repeat and in which years.

  3. 3

    Practise

    Download the repetition report and a guess paper with an answer key. Then ask follow-up questions in your own language.

Frequently asked questions

What is Guess Paper Builder?

It is a free web tool that reads your past exam papers and textbook, counts how often each question repeats across years, and writes a likely guess paper with an answer key.

Is Guess Paper Builder free?

Yes. You only pay your AI provider if you choose a paid model. Groq offers a free tier you can use.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login, signup or subscription.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Files are read in your browser. The only outside request is to the AI provider you choose, made with your own API key.

Which file types can I upload?

Text-based PDF, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, CSV, TXT and Markdown files, up to 20 files per session.

Does it work with scanned papers?

Not reliably. Scanned PDFs need a text layer. Text-based PDFs give the best results.

Is the guess paper guaranteed to appear in the exam?

No. It is a study aid built from patterns in past papers. Use it alongside your textbook and your teacher's guidance.

Which AI providers are supported?

OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq and Anthropic Claude. You paste your own key for the provider you choose.

Why I built this

I was preparing for my intermediate exams and kept facing the same problem. I had around ten past papers from the last five years and my own book, but no clear picture of which questions were coming back. Going through every paper by hand took too long, and I still felt unprepared.

So I built this tool for myself. It reads the papers, finds the questions that keep repeating, and turns them into a guess paper you can practise with. I am sharing it free so that other students can use it too.
